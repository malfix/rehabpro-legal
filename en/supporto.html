How to get support

Write to malfara.labs@gmail.com. A person replies, normally within three working days.

For a technical report it is helpful to state from the outset the version of the application, the iPhone model, the iOS version and what was being done when the problem appeared. There is no need to attach anything concerning a patient — and nothing of the kind should be attached: rehabpro does not collect data of that kind and support does not need them in order to work.

What rehabpro does

It is the tool with which a physiotherapist composes a home-exercise programme from a catalogue included in the application, and hands the patient a printed card of it. It works in the language of the phone, in 21 European languages, with no connection, no account and no patient data.

Frequently asked questions

Where do patients' data end up?

Nowhere, because they are not asked for. There is no field whose function is to identify a patient: no name, no date of birth, no code. The programme that the therapist composes stays on the therapist's own device and is not transmitted to anyone, the provider included. The details are in the privacy policy.

Is an account needed? Is internet needed?

No account. The connection is needed only for purchasing the subscription and for the periodic check with Apple that it is active: composing a programme and printing it works without a network too.

How is the card handed to the patient?

From the composed programme, with Confirm and share: printing and sharing rely on the functions of the operating system, so the same destinations apply as for any other app — printer, PDF, message.

Why do the doses start empty?

It is a design choice, not an oversight: the application neither fills in nor suggests any dose value, and what is not written is not printed. The therapist's usual values are set once in Settings and are shown openly on new exercises, but they count only once they are confirmed.

I have changed iPhone: how do I recover the subscription?

With Restore purchases, in the application's Settings, using the same Apple Account with which it was taken out. Composed programmes, on the other hand, are not transferred and are not synchronised between devices: they remain on the device on which they were written, because there is no server through which they could pass.

How is the subscription cancelled? And refunds?

Cancellation is done from the Apple Account settings (Settings → the user's own name → Subscriptions), at least twenty-four hours before the end of the current period; access continues until the end of the period already paid for. Refunds are handled exclusively by Apple: the provider has no power to grant them. The full terms are in the terms.

The subscription has expired: have I lost the work done?

No. Without an active subscription no new programme can be composed and no programme already composed can be exported, but nothing is deleted: the programme remains saved on the device, not accessible, and returns intact when the subscription is reactivated. The exercise catalogue remains available for consultation.

There is an error in a description, or an exercise is missing

It is worth reporting it to the address above, stating the name of the exercise. The catalogue texts are material supporting composition and are corrected in subsequent versions.

One thing support cannot do

rehabpro is a composition and printing tool: it is not a medical device, it makes no diagnosis and it proposes no therapy. No reply from support constitutes a clinical indication. The choice of exercises, the dosage, the limits and the suitability for the individual patient belong to the physiotherapist who composes the programme, under their professional responsibility.
