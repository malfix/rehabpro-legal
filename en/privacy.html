rehabpro privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

In summary

rehabpro does not collect personal data. It does not require an account to be created, has no server, contains no analytics, statistics, advertising or error-reporting system, and provides no field intended to identify a patient.

The exercise programme that the therapist composes stays on the therapist's own device. It is not transmitted to anyone, the controller included.

The only network communication the application makes is with Apple, to verify whether the subscription is active. That communication carries neither the programme, nor the exercises, nor any text written by the therapist.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Emanuele Malfarà, a natural person, Italy, in the capacity of developer of the rehabpro application.

For any request concerning this policy: malfara.labs@gmail.com.

2. What data the application processes

rehabpro is a tool that a physiotherapist uses to compose a home-exercise programme and hand a printed card of it to their patient. The data the application handles are exclusively the following, and all of them remain on the device:

  • the exercises chosen from the catalogue included in the application, with the doses, limits and notes that the therapist writes;
  • the activation status of the subscription, as communicated by Apple.

There is not, and there is not intended to be, any field whose function is to identify a patient: no name, no date of birth, no code, no free-text heading at programme level. This is not a configuration choice but a property of the product, verified by automated checks on the source code at every change.

The residual risk, declared rather than left unsaid

Some fields the therapist fills in are free text: the pain limit, the load and the note. None of them asks for a patient's data, and nothing in the application prompts for it. A therapist may nonetheless, on their own initiative, write a name or other personal data in them. No product can prevent that.

If this happens, that text remains on the therapist's device and reaches no one else. For that processing the controller is the therapist themselves, within the scope of their professional activity and of the obligations they already owe to their patients. The controller named in this policy has no access to those data, does not receive them and cannot extract them.

The circumstances that contain this risk are three, and they are verifiable: there is only one programme at a time, resetting is explicit and complete, and no copy of the exported card is kept by the application.

3. Where the data are stored, and for how long

In the application's local storage, on the device, and nowhere else. There are no servers, remote backups or cloud services operated by the controller. The data remain until the therapist resets the programme or uninstalls the application; in either case they are removed from the device.

If the therapist has enabled the iCloud backup provided by Apple, the local storage may be included in that backup according to the device settings and the Apple privacy policy. This is processing that takes place between the user and Apple, to which the controller is extraneous.

4. No tracking

rehabpro does not contain, and does not contain in deactivated form:

  • statistics, usage analytics or telemetry systems;
  • automatic error-reporting tools;
  • advertising identifiers, advertising, profiling or automated decision-making;
  • access to the camera or to the photo library;
  • cookies or equivalent technologies.

In the privacy declaration attached to the application, the controller declares to Apple that it collects no category of data and performs no tracking.

5. Subscription and payments

The subscription is purchased through the App Store: the seller is Apple, which handles payment, renewal, any trial period and refund requests. The controller neither receives nor processes the payment card data or the user's Apple Account data.

From Apple the controller receives:

  • aggregated sales reports, which show how many activations took place and in which country, without making it possible to identify a person;
  • any aggregated diagnostic and crash data, and only concerning users who have chosen to share analytics information with developers in their device settings. This sharing is a choice the user makes towards Apple, which can be withdrawn at any time from the iOS settings.

Verifying whether the subscription is active requires a communication between the application and Apple. That communication concerns exclusively the right of use: no data of the programme, the patient or the catalogue pass through it, and this too is verified by an automated check on the source code.

6. Legal basis and purposes

For the data that remain on the device, the processing is carried out by the therapist for their own professional purpose and under their own responsibility. The controller named in this policy determines neither its purposes nor its means beyond providing the tool.

For the subscription status, the legal basis is the performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679): without that verification the application cannot know whether the service is due.

7. Disclosure and transfer of data

No data are disclosed to third parties, disseminated or transferred outside the European Union by the controller, for the simplest of reasons: the controller holds none. The processing carried out by Apple in its capacity as distributor and seller is governed by Apple's privacy policy, linked above.

8. Rights of the data subject

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 grants the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (Articles 15–22). How they apply here must be stated precisely:

  • towards the controller: since the controller holds no personal data of the users or of their patients, there is nothing to extract, rectify or erase. A request is nonetheless answered, and this will be the answer.
  • on the device: control is direct and immediate. The therapist can reset the programme from the application or uninstall it, obtaining complete removal without having to request it from anyone.
  • towards Apple: for account, payment and subscription data, requests must be addressed to Apple.

A complaint may also be lodged with the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

In addition to the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali named above, a complaint may also be lodged with the supervisory authority of the country of habitual residence or place of work, or of the country where the infringement is believed to have occurred (Article 77 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679). 9. Intended users of the application

rehabpro is intended for healthcare professionals. It is not directed at minors and does not knowingly process data of minors.

10. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with a new update date. Should a change concern the nature of the data processed, this will be highlighted at the top.
