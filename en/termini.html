Clinical disclaimer

rehabpro is a composition and printing tool. It is not a medical device, it makes no diagnosis, it proposes no therapy and it in no way replaces the assessment of a healthcare professional.

Clinical judgement belongs entirely to the physiotherapist who composes the programme: the choice of exercises, their dosage, the limits to be observed and the suitability for the individual patient are and remain their professional decision, taken under their responsibility.

The application neither fills in nor suggests any dose value: the fields start empty by design, and what is not written is not printed.

Before handing a card to a patient, the physiotherapist is required to verify that the selected exercises, the descriptions of the movements and the limits indicated are appropriate for that person and for that condition. The descriptive texts included in the catalogue are material supporting composition and do not constitute a clinical indication.

2. Subscription terms

rehabpro is offered by subscription, with weekly, monthly and annual plans. The prices in force are those shown in the application and on the App Store product page at the time of purchase, in the currency and with the VAT applicable to the country of the account.

  • Automatic renewal. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled. The charge is made to the user's Apple Account.
  • Trial period. The annual plan may include a free trial period of one month. The trial is granted by Apple only once per Apple Account; when it expires, unless cancelled, the annual subscription continues on a paid basis.
  • Cancellation. It can be done at any time from the Apple Account settings (Settings → the user's own name → Subscriptions), at least twenty-four hours before the end of the current period. Access continues until the end of the period already paid for.
  • Change of plan. Moving to a higher plan takes effect immediately, with a proportional adjustment handled by Apple; moving to a lower plan takes effect from the next renewal.
  • Refunds. They are handled exclusively by Apple, according to its terms. The provider has no power to grant refunds.

What happens if the subscription is not active

Without an active subscription the application does not allow a new programme to be composed, nor one already composed to be exported. This must be stated clearly, because it is a condition that concerns work already done: a programme composed during the trial period or the subscription is not deleted. It remains saved on the device, not accessible, and returns intact the moment the subscription is reactivated.

The exercise catalogue remains available for consultation. No data are removed from the device as a result of expiry.

3. Requirements and operation

rehabpro works without an internet connection and does not require an account to be created. A connection is needed only for the purchase and for the periodic verification of the subscription with Apple.

Printing and sharing the card rely on the functions of the device's operating system.

4. Data processing

The application does not collect personal data and provides no fields intended to identify a patient. The arrangements are described in the privacy policy, which forms an integral part of these notices.
